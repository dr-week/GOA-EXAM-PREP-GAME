### Question 1
**What is the name of the 3rd administrative district created in Goa in January 2026?**
- A) Sattari District
- B) Kushavati District (HQ: Quepem)
- C) Ponda District
- D) Pernem District

**Answer:** B) Kushavati District (HQ: Quepem)
**Explanation:** In January 2026, the Government of Goa created the 3rd administrative district named Kushavati (headquartered at Quepem), carved out of South Goa comprising Dharbandora, Quepem, Sanguem, and Canacona talukas.

### Question 2
**Under the ULLAS initiative in 2025, Goa achieved which landmark educational status in India?**
- A) First state to adopt AI curriculum
- B) First state to achieve 100% functional literacy
- C) Highest number of medical colleges per capita
- D) First state to introduce 4-year primary education

**Answer:** B) First state to achieve 100% functional literacy
**Explanation:** Goa became the first Indian state to achieve 100% functional literacy under the Union Government's ULLAS (Understanding Lifelong Learning for All in Society) initiative.

### Question 3
**Which traditional Goan beverage holds a official Geographical Indication (GI) Tag?**
- A) Kokum Sharbat
- B) Cashew Feni
- C) Sol Kadi
- D) Urrak

**Answer:** B) Cashew Feni
**Explanation:** Goa Cashew Feni was granted a Geographical Indication (GI) tag in 2009, recognizing it as a unique heritage drink of Goa produced from cashew apples.

### Question 5
**What is the peak elevation of Sonsogor, the highest mountain peak in the state of Goa?**
- A) 850 meters
- B) 1,020 meters
- C) 1,166 meters
- D) 1,450 meters

**Answer:** C) 1,166 meters
**Explanation:** Sonsogor (also known as Sonagaddi) is the highest peak in Goa, standing at 1,166 meters (3,825 feet) above sea level in the Western Ghats (Sattari taluka).

### Question 8
**What is the total geographical area of the state of Goa?**
- A) 2,500 sq. km
- B) 3,702 sq. km
- C) 5,100 sq. km
- D) 7,200 sq. km

**Answer:** B) 3,702 sq. km
**Explanation:** Goa is India's smallest state by land area, covering 3,702 square kilometers (1,429 sq miles), bounded by Maharashtra to the north and Karnataka to the east and south.

### Question 9
**Which river flows near the famous ancient Rock Carvings of Usgalimal in South Goa?**
- A) Chapora River
- B) Kushavati River
- C) Sal River
- D) Talpona River

**Answer:** B) Kushavati River
**Explanation:** The prehistoric petroglyphs at Usgalimal (dating back over 10,000 years to the Upper Paleolithic/Mesolithic era) are located on the banks of the Kushavati River near Rivona.
