### Question 1
**If the price of sugar increases by 20%, by what percentage must a household reduce its consumption so that total expenditure remains unchanged?**
- A) 15%
- B) 16.67%
- C) 20%
- D) 25%

**Answer:** B) 16.67%
**Explanation:** 
Formula: `Percentage reduction = [R / (100 + R)] × 100%`
Here R = 20%.
`Reduction = [20 / 120] × 100 = 100 / 6 = 16.67%`.

### Question 2
**A train 150 meters long is running at a speed of 54 km/hr. How much time will it take to cross a platform 250 meters long?**
- A) 20 seconds
- B) 24.67 seconds
- C) 26.67 seconds
- D) 30 seconds

**Answer:** C) 26.67 seconds
**Explanation:** 
Total distance to cover = Length of train + Length of platform = 150 + 250 = 400 meters.
Speed in m/s = 54 × (5/18) = 15 m/s.
Time = Distance / Speed = 400 / 15 = 26.67 seconds.

### Question 3
**A person sold an article at a profit of 15%. If he had bought it at 10% less and sold it for ₹18 less, he would have gained 25%. Find the cost price of the article.**
- A) ₹600
- B) ₹720
- C) ₹800
- D) ₹900

**Answer:** B) ₹720
**Explanation:** 
Let CP = 100x. SP1 = 115x.
New CP = 90x. New SP2 = 90x × 1.25 = 112.5x.
Difference = 115x - 112.5x = 2.5x.
Given 2.5x = 18 => x = 7.2 => 100x = ₹720.

### Question 4
**A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. B alone can finish it in 30 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?**
- A) 18 days
- B) 20 days
- C) 22 days
- D) 25 days

**Answer:** B) 20 days
**Explanation:** 
1 day work of (A + B) = 1/12. 1 day work of B = 1/30.
1 day work of A = (1/12) - (1/30) = (5 - 2)/60 = 3/60 = 1/20.
Therefore, A alone can finish in 20 days.

### Question 5
**What will be the compound interest on ₹10,000 for 2 years at 10% per annum compounded annually?**
- A) ₹2,000
- B) ₹2,100
- C) ₹2,200
- D) ₹2,500

**Answer:** B) ₹2,100
**Explanation:** 
Amount = P(1 + r/100)^t = 10000 × (1.1)² = 10000 × 1.21 = ₹12,100.
CI = Amount - Principal = 12100 - 10000 = ₹2,100.

### Question 6
**[Data Interpretation] The average production of wheat in a farm over 4 consecutive years was 120 tons. If the production in the 5th year was 170 tons, what is the new 5-year average?**
- A) 125 tons
- B) 130 tons
- C) 135 tons
- D) 140 tons

**Answer:** B) 130 tons
**Explanation:** 
Total production of 4 years = 120 × 4 = 480 tons.
Total production of 5 years = 480 + 170 = 650 tons.
New average = 650 / 5 = 130 tons.
