# Goa Special General Knowledge - Questions & Answers

High-yield question bank for **GPSC, GSSC, LDC, MTS, and Goa Government Exams**.

---

### Question 1
**In which year was Goa liberated from Portuguese rule by Operation Vijay?**
- A) 1947
- B) 1950
- C) 1961
- D) 1987

**Answer:** C) 1961
**Explanation:** Indian armed forces launched Operation Vijay on December 18, 1961, which led to the liberation of Goa, Daman, and Diu from Portuguese rule on December 19, 1961. December 19 is celebrated annually as Goa Liberation Day.

---

### Question 2
**Which river is known as the "Lifeline of Goa"?**
- A) Zuari
- B) Mandovi (Mahadayi)
- C) Sal
- D) Chapora

**Answer:** B) Mandovi (Mahadayi)
**Explanation:** The Mandovi River (Mahadayi) originates in the Western Ghats at Bhimgad and flows across Goa into the Arabian Sea, supplying fresh water to the majority of the state.

---

### Question 3
**On which date did Goa officially become the 25th state of India?**
- A) 19 December 1961
- B) 30 May 1987
- C) 26 January 1950
- D) 15 August 1947

**Answer:** B) 30 May 1987
**Explanation:** Goa was conferred full statehood on May 30, 1987. May 30 is celebrated as Goa Statehood Day.

---

### Question 4
**Which is the official language of the state of Goa under the Goa Official Language Act, 1987?**
- A) Marathi
- B) Konkani (in Devanagari script)
- C) Portuguese
- D) English

**Answer:** B) Konkani (in Devanagari script)
**Explanation:** Under the Goa Official Language Act 1987, Konkani in the Devanagari script is the sole official language of Goa.

---

### Question 5
**The famous Dudhsagar Waterfalls is located on which river?**
- A) Zuari
- B) Mandovi
- C) Tiracol
- D) Talpona

**Answer:** B) Mandovi
**Explanation:** Dudhsagar Falls is a four-tiered waterfall located on the Mandovi River inside Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary and Mollem National Park.

### Question 6
**Who was the first Chief Minister of Goa, Daman and Diu?**
- A) Dayanand Bandodkar
- B) Jack de Sequeira
- C) Pratapsingh Rane
- D) Manohar Parrikar

**Answer:** A) Dayanand Bandodkar
**Explanation:** Dayanand Bandodkar (Bhausaheb Bandodkar) of the Maharashtrawadi Gomantak Party (MGP) served as the first Chief Minister of Goa from 1963 to 1973.

### Question 7
**Which sanctuary is the largest wildlife sanctuary in Goa?**
- A) Dr. Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary
- B) Bondla Wildlife Sanctuary
- C) Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary
- D) Cotigao Wildlife Sanctuary

**Answer:** C) Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary
**Explanation:** Located in Mollem, Bhagwan Mahaveer Wildlife Sanctuary covers 240 sq km and is the largest wildlife sanctuary in Goa.

### Question 8
**The Historic Opinion Poll of 1967 in Goa was held to decide:**
- A) Whether Goa should be divided into North and South districts
- B) Whether Goa should merge with Maharashtra or remain a Union Territory
- C) The state capital location
- D) Adoption of Portuguese vs Indian Civil Code

**Answer:** B) Whether Goa should merge with Maharashtra or remain a Union Territory
**Explanation:** Held on January 16, 1967, the referendum voted decisively for Goa to remain an independent Union Territory rather than merge with Maharashtra.

### Question 9
**Following recent administrative reorganization, how many total administrative districts does Goa have?**
- A) 2 Districts
- B) 3 Districts (North Goa, South Goa, and Kushavati)
- C) 4 Districts
- D) 5 Districts

**Answer:** B) 3 Districts (North Goa, South Goa, and Kushavati)
**Explanation:** Goa has 3 administrative districts: North Goa (HQ: Panaji), South Goa (HQ: Margao), and Kushavati district (HQ: Quepem, carved out of South Goa comprising Dharbandora, Quepem, Sanguem, and Canacona talukas). Source: Government of Goa Official Gazette.

### Question 10
**Which UNESCO World Heritage Site in Goa houses the mortal remains of St. Francis Xavier?**
- A) Church of St. Cajetan
- B) Basilica of Bom Jesus
- C) Se Cathedral
- D) Church of Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception

**Answer:** B) Basilica of Bom Jesus
**Explanation:** Located in Old Goa, the Basilica of Bom Jesus holds the preserved mortal remains of St. Francis Xavier.

### Question 11
**What is the length of the newly constructed 8-lane cable-stayed bridge over the Zuari River in Goa (New Zuari Bridge)?**
- A) 320 meters
- B) 640 meters
- C) 1.08 km
- D) 2.5 km

**Answer:** B) 640 meters
**Explanation:** The New Zuari Bridge is India's second longest cable-stayed bridge, spanning 640 meters across the Zuari River connecting North and South Goa.

### Question 12
**Manohar International Airport at Mopa in North Goa was developed in partnership with which infrastructure group?**
- A) Adani Group
- B) GMR Group
- C) Tata Infrastructure
- D) Reliance Infrastructure

**Answer:** B) GMR Group
**Explanation:** Manohar International Airport (Mopa) was developed by GMR Goa International Airport Limited (GGIAL).
