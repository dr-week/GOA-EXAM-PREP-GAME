High-yield question bank for **GPSC, GSSC, LDC, MTS, and Goa Government Exams**.

### Question 1
**What is the famous traditional spring festival celebrated with vibrant parades and floats across major cities in Goa?**
- A) Shigmo Festival (Shigmotsav)
- B) Sao Joao
- C) Bonderam
- D) Chikhal Kalo

**Answer:** A) Shigmo Festival (Shigmotsav)
**Explanation:** Shigmo (Shigmotsav) is the traditional Hindu spring festival in Goa celebrating rich folklore, traditional dances (like Ghode Modni, Fugdi), and float parades.

---

### Question 2
**The festival of 'Bonderam' featuring colorful flag parades is celebrated on which island in Goa?**
- A) Chorao Island
- B) Divar Island
- C) St. Jacinto Island
- D) Vanxim Island

**Answer:** B) Divar Island
**Explanation:** Bonderam is celebrated every August on Divar Island, reenacting historical boundary disputes using colorful flags.

### Question 3
**Which welfare scheme launched by the Government of Goa provides monthly financial assistance to homemakers/women?**
- A) Ladli Laxmi Scheme
- B) Griha Aadhar Scheme
- C) Deen Dayal Swasthya Seva Yojana
- D) Cyber Age Scheme

**Answer:** B) Griha Aadhar Scheme
**Explanation:** Griha Aadhar provides monthly financial support to housewives/homemakers from middle and lower-income families in Goa.

---

### Question 4
**What is the name of the traditional Goan mud-games festival celebrated in Marcel to honor Lord Krishna?**
- A) Sao Joao
- B) Chikhal Kalo
- C) Shigmo
- D) Tripurari Poornima

**Answer:** B) Chikhal Kalo
**Explanation:** Chikhal Kalo (mud festival) is a unique traditional monsoon festival celebrated in Marcel village, where people play traditional games in mud.

### Question 5
**Which crop is celebrated during the famous 'Harvest Festival' (Consua / Konsamchem Fest) in Goa?**
- A) Cashew
- B) Coconut
- C) Paddy (Rice)
- D) Mango

**Answer:** C) Paddy (Rice)
**Explanation:** Konsamchem Fest / Harvest Festival in August celebrates the blessing of the first corn/paddy crop of the season.
