# Goa Geography, Rivers, Ports & Wildlife - Questions & Answers

High-yield question bank for **GPSC, GSSC, LDC, MTS, and Goa Government Exams**.

---

### Question 1
**Mormugao Port, the major seaport of Goa, is situated at the mouth of which river?**
- A) Mandovi River
- B) Zuari River
- C) Chapora River
- D) Sal River

**Answer:** B) Zuari River
**Explanation:** Mormugao Port is a major natural harbor in South Goa located at the entrance/mouth of the Zuari River.

---

### Question 2
**Which mountain range forms the eastern boundary of the state of Goa?**
- A) Vindhya Range
- B) Western Ghats (Sahyadri Range)
- C) Eastern Ghats
- D) Satpura Range

**Answer:** B) Western Ghats (Sahyadri Range)
**Explanation:** The Western Ghats (Sahyadris) form the eastern boundary of Goa, providing rich tropical rainforests, rivers, and Western Ghats biodiversity.

---

### Question 3
**Which is the highest peak in the state of Goa?**
- A) Sonsogor (Sonsogodd)
- B) Vagheri
- C) Sidhanath
- D) Chandranath

**Answer:** A) Sonsogor (Sonsogodd)
**Explanation:** Sonsogor (Sonsogodd) in the Sattari taluka is the highest peak in Goa, situated at an altitude of 1,166 meters in the Western Ghats.

---

### Question 4
**Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary in Goa is located on which island?**
- A) Divar Island
- B) Chorao Island
- C) St. Jacinto Island
- D) Tiswadi Island

**Answer:** B) Chorao Island
**Explanation:** Named after ornithologist Dr. Salim Ali, this estuarine mangrove sanctuary is located along the Mandovi River on Chorao Island.

---

### Question 5
**Which is the smallest wildlife sanctuary in Goa by area?**
- A) Bondla Wildlife Sanctuary
- B) Cotigao Wildlife Sanctuary
- C) Netravali Wildlife Sanctuary
- D) Madei Wildlife Sanctuary

**Answer:** A) Bondla Wildlife Sanctuary
**Explanation:** Bondla Wildlife Sanctuary in Ponda taluka is the smallest sanctuary in Goa, covering an area of 8 square kilometers.
