**[QUANTITATIVE_APTITUDE] A train 150 meters long is running at a speed of 54 km/hr. How much time will it take to cross a platform 250 meters long?**
- A) 20 seconds
- B) 26.67 seconds
- C) 24.67 seconds
- D) 30 seconds

**Answer:** B) 26.67 seconds
**Explanation:** Total distance to cover = Length of train + Length of platform = 150 + 250 = 400 meters.
Speed in m/s = 54 × (5/18) = 15 m/s.
Time = Distance / Speed = 400 / 15 = 26.67 seconds.

**[QUANTITATIVE_APTITUDE] A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. B alone can finish it in 30 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?**
- A) 18 days
- B) 25 days
- C) 20 days
- D) 22 days

**Answer:** C) 20 days
**Explanation:** 1 day work of (A + B) = 1/12. 1 day work of B = 1/30.
1 day work of A = (1/12) - (1/30) = (5 - 2)/60 = 3/60 = 1/20.
Therefore, A alone can finish in 20 days.
