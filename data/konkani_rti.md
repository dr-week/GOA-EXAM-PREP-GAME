# Konkani Language & Goa RTI Act - Questions & Answers

High-yield question bank for **GPSC, GSSC, LDC, MTS, and Goa Government Exams**.

---

### Question 1
**Under the Goa Official Language Act 1987, which language is the official language of the state?**
- A) Marathi
- B) Konkani in Devanagari script
- C) Portuguese
- D) English

**Answer:** B) Konkani in Devanagari script
**Explanation:** The Goa Official Language Act of 1987 states that Konkani written in the Devanagari script is the sole official language of Goa.

---

### Question 2
**What is the maximum time limit for a Public Information Officer (PIO) to supply information under the RTI Act 2005 for ordinary requests?**
- A) 15 days
- B) 30 days
- C) 45 days
- D) 60 days

**Answer:** B) 30 days
**Explanation:** Under Section 7(1) of the RTI Act 2005, the PIO must provide the requested information within 30 days of receiving the application.

---

### Question 3
**If the information sought under RTI concerns the life or liberty of a person, within how many hours must it be provided?**
- A) 24 hours
- B) 48 hours
- C) 72 hours
- D) 7 days

**Answer:** B) 48 hours
**Explanation:** Proviso to Section 7(1) mandates that information concerning life or liberty must be provided within 48 hours.

### Question 4
**In Konkani grammar, what does the word 'उदक' (Udak) mean?**
- A) Milk
- B) Water
- C) Fire
- D) Earth

**Answer:** B) Water
**Explanation:** In Konkani, 'उदक' (Udak) translates to Water.

### Question 5
**What is the primary portal for applying to Goa Government online services and certificates?**
- A) Digital Goa
- B) Goa Online (goaonline.gov.in)
- C) Goa Seva
- D) E-Goa Portal

**Answer:** B) Goa Online (goaonline.gov.in)
**Explanation:** Goa Online (goaonline.gov.in) is the official single-window e-governance portal for Goa State Government services.
