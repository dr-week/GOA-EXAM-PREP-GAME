### Question 1
**[GitHub Repo: ratneshmaurya/Aptitude_Practice_Questions] What is the HCF of 24, 36, and 60?**
- A) 6
- B) 12
- C) 18
- D) 24

**Answer:** B) 12
**Explanation:** 
Factors of 24 = 2³ × 3
Factors of 36 = 2² × 3²
Factors of 60 = 2² × 3 × 5
HCF = 2² × 3 = 12.

- A) 30
- B) 60
- C) 90
- D) 120

**Answer:** B) 60
**Explanation:** 
Prime factors: 12 = 2² × 3, 15 = 3 × 5, 20 = 2² × 5.
LCM = 2² × 3 × 5 = 60.

### Question 3
**[GitHub Repo: drsarveshwarbharti/Aptitude-For-Placements] If A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 4:5, what is A:B:C?**
- A) 8:12:15
- B) 6:9:15
- C) 8:10:15
- D) 2:4:5

**Answer:** A) 8:12:15
**Explanation:** 
Multiply A:B by 4 -> 8:12.
Multiply B:C by 3 -> 12:15.
Combined ratio A:B:C = 8:12:15.

### Question 4
**[GitHub Repo: rohanmistry231/CSE-Aptitude-Test-Practice-Hub] A sum of money doubles itself at simple interest in 8 years. What is the rate of interest per annum?**
- A) 10%
- B) 12.5%
- C) 15%
- D) 16.67%

**Answer:** B) 12.5%
**Explanation:** 
SI = Principal = P. Time = 8 years.
Formula: `SI = (P × R × T) / 100` => `P = (P × R × 8) / 100` => `R = 100 / 8 = 12.5%`.
