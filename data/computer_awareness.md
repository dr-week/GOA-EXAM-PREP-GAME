### Question 1
**In MS Excel, which function key is used to edit the contents of a selected cell?**
- A) F1
- B) F2
- C) F4
- D) F7

**Answer:** B) F2
**Explanation:** Pressing F2 allows you to directly edit the active cell in Microsoft Excel.

---

### Question 2
**What is the shortcut key to permanently delete a file without moving it to the Recycle Bin in Windows?**
- A) Ctrl + Delete
- B) Shift + Delete
- C) Alt + Delete
- D) Ctrl + Shift + Delete

**Answer:** B) Shift + Delete
**Explanation:** Shift + Delete bypasses the Recycle Bin and permanently deletes the selected file or folder.

---

### Question 3
**Which protocol is used for secure communication over the Internet?**
- A) HTTP
- B) HTTPS
- C) FTP
- D) SMTP

**Answer:** B) HTTPS
**Explanation:** HTTPS (Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure) encrypts data sent between your browser and the website using SSL/TLS.

---

### Question 4
**What is the name of the official digital land records portal of the Government of Goa?**
- A) Bhulekh Goa
- B) Dharani Goa / Goa Land Records Portal
- C) GoaOnline
- D) AnyRoR Goa

**Answer:** B) Dharani Goa / Goa Land Records Portal
**Explanation:** Land records, Form I & XIV, and mutation status in Goa are managed digitally via the Directorate of Settlement and Land Records portal.

---

### Question 5
**What type of network covers a small geographical area such as an office building or school?**
- A) WAN
- B) LAN
- C) MAN
- D) PAN

**Answer:** B) LAN
**Explanation:** Local Area Network (LAN) connects computers across a short physical distance like an office or school.
