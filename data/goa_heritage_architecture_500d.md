### Question 1
**[GOA_HERITAGE_H1] The famous Mangueshi Temple at Priol features which unique Goan architectural lamp tower?**
- A) Deepastambha (Deepstambh)
- B) Gopuram
- C) Shikhara
- D) Minaret

**Answer:** A) Deepastambha (Deepstambh)
**Explanation:** Traditional Goan Hindu temples feature multi-storied octagonal lamp towers known as Deepastambha (Deepstambh).
