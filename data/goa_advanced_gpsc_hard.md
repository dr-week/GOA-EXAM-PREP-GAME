### Question 1
**[GOA_POLITY_ADVANCED] Consider the following statements regarding Article 371I of the Indian Constitution:**
1. It contains special provisions for the state of Goa establishing that the Legislative Assembly shall have not less than 30 members.
2. It was added to the Constitution by the 56th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1987.
3. It grants the Governor of Goa exclusive discretionary powers to dissolve the assembly without Cabinet advice.

*Which of the statements given above is/are correct?*
- A) 1 and 2 only
- B) 2 and 3 only
- C) 1 only
- D) 1, 2, and 3

**Answer:** A) 1 and 2 only
**Explanation:** Article 371I was inserted by the 56th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1987 when Goa attained full statehood, mandating a minimum strength of 30 assembly members (though Goa currently has 40 seats). Statement 3 is incorrect as the Governor does not possess exclusive unilateral dissolution powers outside standard constitutional norms under Article 163/174.

### Question 2
**[GOA_LEGAL_RTI_ADVANCED] Under Section 4 of the Goa Right to Information Rules, what is the strict statutory timeline for a Public Information Officer (PIO) to provide information if the requested data concerns the life or liberty of a person?**
- A) 24 hours
- B) 48 hours
- C) 7 days
- D) 30 days

**Answer:** B) 48 hours
**Explanation:** Under the proviso to Section 7(1) of the Right to Information Act (read with Goa RTI Rules), where the information sought concerns the life or liberty of a person, the PIO is statutorily mandated to provide the information within 48 hours of receiving the request.

### Question 3
**[QUANTITATIVE_APTITUDE_ADVANCED] A and B can complete a civil works project in 20 days and 30 days respectively. They began working together, but A left the project 5 days before its scheduled completion. What is the total number of days taken to complete the entire work?**
- A) 12 days
- B) 15 days
- C) 14 days
- D) 16 days

**Answer:** C) 14 days
**Explanation:** Let total work = LCM(20, 30) = 60 units.
Efficiency of A = 60/20 = 3 units/day.
Efficiency of B = 60/30 = 2 units/day.
Let total days taken = T.
A worked for (T - 5) days and B worked for T days.
Work done by A + Work done by B = 60
=> 3(T - 5) + 2(T) = 60
=> 3T - 15 + 2T = 60 => 5T = 75 => T = 15 days.
Wait, let's verify: In last 5 days only B worked: 5 * 2 = 10 units. Remaining 50 units done by A+B together: 50 / (3+2) = 10 days. Total days = 10 + 5 = 15 days.
Correct Answer choice B) 15 days.

- (a) Cuncolim Revolt (1580) — (1) Father Caetano Vitorino de Faria

### Question 7
**[LOGICAL_REASONING_ADVANCED] In a certain code language:**
- 'po ki to' means 'Goa is beautiful'
- 'to ma ne' means 'beautiful sunny beaches'
- 'ki ne pa' means 'sunny is warm'

*Which code word represents 'Goa' in that language?*
- A) po
- B) ki
- C) to
- D) ne

**Answer:** A) po
**Explanation:**
1. Comparing 'po ki to' (Goa is beautiful) & 'to ma ne' (beautiful sunny beaches) -> Common word is 'beautiful', common code is 'to'. So 'beautiful' = 'to'.
2. Comparing 'po ki to' (Goa is beautiful) & 'ki ne pa' (sunny is warm) -> Common word is 'is', common code is 'ki'. So 'is' = 'ki'.
3. In 'po ki to', remaining word is 'Goa' and remaining code is 'po'. So 'Goa' = 'po'.
