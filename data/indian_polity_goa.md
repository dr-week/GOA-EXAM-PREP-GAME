# Indian Constitution & Polity (Goa Exam Special) - Questions & Answers

High-yield question bank for **GPSC, GSSC, LDC, MTS, and Goa State Level Examinations**.

---

### Question 1
**Which Article of the Constitution of India contains special provisions with respect to the State of Goa?**
- A) Article 370
- B) Article 371I
- C) Article 371A
- D) Article 371J

**Answer:** B) Article 371I
**Explanation:** Article 371I specifies that the Legislative Assembly of the State of Goa shall consist of not less than thirty members.

---

### Question 2
**Under the Panchayati Raj system in Goa, what are the two tiers of rural local self-government?**
- A) Gram Panchayat and Zilla Panchayat
- B) Gram Panchayat and Panchayat Samiti
- C) Zilla Panchayat and Municipal Council
- D) Gram Sabha and Nyaya Panchayat

**Answer:** A) Gram Panchayat and Zilla Panchayat
**Explanation:** Goa follows a two-tier Panchayati Raj system consisting of Village (Gram) Panchayats at the base and Zilla Panchayats (North Goa & South Goa Zilla Panchayats) at the district level.

### Question 3
**How many seats are there in the Goa State Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha)?**
- A) 30
- B) 40
- C) 60
- D) 70

**Answer:** B) 40
**Explanation:** The Goa Legislative Assembly consists of 40 elected members (MLAs).

---

### Question 4
**How many members represent the State of Goa in the Lok Sabha (Lower House of Parliament)?**
- A) 1 Member
- B) 2 Members
- C) 3 Members
- D) 4 Members

**Answer:** B) 2 Members
**Explanation:** Goa is divided into 2 Lok Sabha parliamentary constituencies: North Goa and South Goa.

---

### Question 5
**How many seats does Goa have in the Rajya Sabha (Upper House of Parliament)?**
- A) 1 Seat
- B) 2 Seats
- C) 3 Seats
- D) 5 Seats

**Answer:** A) 1 Seat
**Explanation:** Goa is represented by 1 seat in the Rajya Sabha.
