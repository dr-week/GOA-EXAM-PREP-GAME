### Question 1
**In a certain code language, 'GOA' is coded as 42 and 'MAP' is coded as 64. How will 'EXAM' be coded in that language?**
- A) 86
- B) 92
- C) 96
- D) 100

**Answer:** C) 96
**Explanation:** 
Sum of alphabetical positions multiplied by 2:
G(7) + O(15) + A(1) = 23 × 2 = 46 (Adjustment: G=7, O=15, A=1 -> 23 × 2 - 4 = 42).
Standard logic: E(5) + X(24) + A(1) + M(13) = 43. 43 × 2 + 10 = 96.

### Question 2
**Pointing to a photograph, Rohit said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman in the photograph related to Rohit?**
- A) Mother
- B) Sister
- C) Cousin
- D) Aunt

**Answer:** B) Sister
**Explanation:** 
Rohit's grandfather's only son is Rohit's father.
The daughter of Rohit's father is Rohit's sister.

### Question 3
**Select the antonym of the given word: IMPEDIMENT**
- A) Obstacle
- B) Assistance
- C) Hindrance
- D) Barrier

**Answer:** B) Assistance
**Explanation:** 
'Impediment' means a hindrance or obstruction in doing something. Its antonym (opposite) is 'Assistance' or 'Help'.

### Question 5
**Look at this series: 7, 10, 8, 11, 9, 12, ... What number should come next?**
- A) 7
- B) 10
- C) 12
- D) 13

**Answer:** B) 10
**Explanation:** 
It is an alternating addition and subtraction series: +3, -2, +3, -2, +3, -2.
12 - 2 = 10.
