### Question 1
**[ADVANCED_GEO_G1] Which estuarine tributary or canal system #1 connects the Mandovi River basin to the Zuari River basin in Goa's Tiswadi region?**
- A) Cumbarjua Canal
- B) Talpona River Reach
- C) Galgibaga Estuary
- D) Chapora Estuary
