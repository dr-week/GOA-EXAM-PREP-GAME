### Question 1
**Who led the Cuncolim Revolt of 1580, recognized as the first freedom struggle against Portuguese rule in Goa?**
- A) Kusti Desai
- B) Chieftains of Cuncolim (Desais of Cuncolim)
- C) Pinto Revolt Leaders
- D) Dayanand Bandodkar

**Answer:** B) Chieftains of Cuncolim (Desais of Cuncolim)
**Explanation:** The Cuncolim Revolt in 1580 was led by the local village chieftains against Portuguese religious oppression, making it one of the earliest armed resistances against European colonialism in Asia.

### Question 2
**The Pinto Revolt (Pinto Conspiracy) of 1787 was led by native Goan priests against Portuguese rule. In which town did it originate?**
- A) Margao
- B) Candolim (Bardez)
- C) Mapusa
- D) Vasco da Gama

**Answer:** B) Candolim (Bardez)
**Explanation:** Led by Father Caetano Vitorino de Faria and Father Jose Antonio Goncalves of Candolim, the 1787 conspiracy aimed to overthrow Portuguese rule.

### Question 3
**Who is fondly remembered as the "Father of Goa's Freedom Movement" for starting the civil disobedience movement on 18th June 1946 in Margao?**
- A) Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia
- B) Dr. Julião Menezes
- C) T.B. Cunha (Tristão de Bragança Cunha)
- D) Vishwanath Lawande

**Answer:** C) T.B. Cunha (Tristão de Bragança Cunha)
**Explanation:** T.B. Cunha founded the Goa Congress Committee in 1928 and wrote famous nationalist booklets, earning him the title 'Father of Goa's Freedom Movement'. June 18 is celebrated as Goa Revolution Day (Goa Kranti Din) after Dr. Lohia & Dr. Menezes addressed the historic rally in Margao.

### Question 4
**Which Indian nationalist leader addressed the historic public meeting in Margao on June 18, 1946, sparking the final phase of Goa's freedom struggle?**
- A) Mahatma Gandhi
- B) Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia
- C) Jawaharlal Nehru
- D) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

**Answer:** B) Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia
**Explanation:** Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia, accompanied by Dr. Julião Menezes, defied Portuguese ban orders on 18th June 1946 in Margao, marking June 18 as Goa Revolution Day.

### Question 5
**What was the name of the armed revolutionary group formed in 1947 by Vishwanath Lawande and comrades to fight Portuguese colonial forces?**
- A) Azad Gomantak Dal (AGD)
- B) Goa Liberation Army
- C) Gomantak Mukti Sena
- D) National Congress (Goa)

**Answer:** A) Azad Gomantak Dal (AGD)
**Explanation:** Azad Gomantak Dal (AGD) engaged in armed guerrilla warfare against Portuguese strongholds, raiding police stations and railway lines until Goa's liberation in 1961.
